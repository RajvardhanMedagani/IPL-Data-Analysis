IPL PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS – POWER BI DAX MEASURES

Total Matches = COUNTROWS(IPL_Cleaned)
Total Runs = SUM(IPL_Cleaned[Total_Runs])
Total Wickets = SUM(IPL_Cleaned[Wickets])
Total Teams = DISTINCTCOUNT(IPL_Cleaned[Winner])
Total Players = DISTINCTCOUNT(IPL_Cleaned[Player_of_Match])
Average Runs per Match = AVERAGE(IPL_Cleaned[Total_Runs])

Matches Won After Toss =
CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(IPL_Cleaned), IPL_Cleaned[Toss_Won_Match] = 1)

Toss-to-Match Win % =
DIVIDE([Matches Won After Toss], [Total Matches], 0)

Recommended slicers: Season, Venue, Winner, Toss_Decision.
